Privacy Policy

ScrapLog · Effective date: September 25, 2026

In short: ScrapLog works entirely on your device. There is no account, no server and no analytics; subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not collect, receive or store your photos, videos, notes or any other data. Your captures are kept in the app’s private storage, are not saved to your Photos library and are not included in iCloud or computer backups.

1. Who we are

ScrapLog is provided by Armin Miranda (AMRV), Werkstattstraße 12, 50733 Köln, Germany (“we”, “us”). He is the controller responsible for any processing of personal data described in this policy. Contact: amrvco@gmail.com. We are not required to appoint a data protection officer.

2. Data the app handles — on your device only

To do its job, the app stores the following on your device:

  • Visits you create (visit name and date), each containing its piles.
  • Photos and videos you capture in the app, including audio recorded with videos, plus any drawings and comments you add to photos (the original photo is kept unchanged next to the marked-up copy).
  • Pile details you enter: pile names, metal type, ISRI grade, specs and notes.
  • A company logo, if you choose one for your PDF reports. The image you pick is copied into the app’s storage in a downsized form; it is not uploaded, and it appears only in the reports you export yourself.
  • App settings, such as your current visit and your favorite ISRI grades.

This data stays in the app’s private storage area on your device. It is not uploaded, synced or transmitted to us or to any third party by the app. We have no access to it.

Photos contain standard technical camera metadata (for example capture time and camera settings). The app does not request access to your location, so no GPS location is added to your captures.

3. Not saved to your Photos library

Captures are deliberately kept separate from your personal photos. The app never writes to your Photos library and does not request access to it. When you share or export, captures are handed over as plain files, so iOS does not offer “Save to Photos”, “Shared Albums” or other photo-library options. As a result, captures are also not included in iCloud Photos — unless you yourself save them there from another app you shared them to.

4. Device permissions

  • Camera — to take photos and videos of piles. Used only while the capture screen is open.
  • Microphone — requested the first time you record a video, and used only while a video is recording so you can narrate. If you decline, videos are recorded without sound.

While the camera is open, the volume buttons (and the Camera Control button where available) act as a shutter. The app only receives the button press itself; this does not involve any personal data.

You can change these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

5. Exporting and sharing

Data leaves the app only when you choose to export or share it — for example a PDF report, a ZIP with all files, or individual files via “Share”. PDF reports contain the visit and pile details, photo comments, (downsized) photos and, if you set one, your company logo. You then pick the destination in the iOS share sheet — such as the Files app, AirDrop, email or a messaging app. From that point on, the recipient and the service or app you chose are responsible for the data, under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive a copy. Temporary files created for an export are deleted automatically.

6. Purchases (ScrapLog Pro)

ScrapLog Pro subscriptions are purchased through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment and your Apple Account details under Apple’s own privacy policy; we do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app checks whether a subscription is active directly on your device using Apple’s StoreKit (signed transaction information provided by Apple). We do not run a server for this and do not link purchases to your content. Apple provides us only with sales reports that do not identify you.

7. No tracking, analytics or advertising

The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

8. Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it: individual captures, piles or whole visits (“Delete” in the visit list), or by deleting the app, which removes all of its data. Starting a new visit does not delete earlier visits. Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore deleted data.

9. Device backups

The iOS app excludes your visits, captures and details from iCloud Backup and from computer backups, so they never leave the device through a backup. As a consequence, if your device is lost, reset or replaced, or the app is deleted, this data cannot be restored — export visits you want to keep. Small app settings (such as favorites) may still be included in device backups by iOS.

10. Web version

If you use ScrapLog in a web browser instead of the iOS app, your captures and details are stored in your browser’s storage on your device (IndexedDB) and are not uploaded. When the web app is loaded, the provider hosting the website may process standard technical connection data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the files. In the browser, the share options are controlled by your browser and operating system.

Camera and microphone access is used only after you allow it in iOS, and you can withdraw that permission at any time in the iOS Settings app. The only personal data we process ourselves is described in sections 11 and 12, together with the legal bases stated there.
