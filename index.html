FAQ

Where are my captures stored?

Everything is stored privately on your device. Captures are never uploaded to a server or added to your Photos library.

How do I export my visit?

Tap the Save button (top right) to export the whole visit as a ZIP file. You can share it via AirDrop, Files, WhatsApp, email, or any other app.

I tapped "New Visit" and lost my captures — can I recover them?

New Visit permanently wipes the current session from the device. Captures cannot be recovered. Export regularly via Save before starting a new visit.

Does ScrapLog work without an internet connection?

Yes — ScrapLog is fully offline. No internet connection is required to capture, organize, or export.

What data does ScrapLog collect?

None. ScrapLog has no backend, no analytics, and no account system. Your captures stay on your device.
