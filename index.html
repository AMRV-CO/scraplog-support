FAQ

Where are my captures stored?

Everything is stored privately on your device. Captures are never uploaded to a server or added to your Photos library.

How do I export my visit?

Open the pile overview and tap Export. You can export the whole visit as a ZIP file (one folder per pile) or, with ScrapLog Pro, as a PDF report. Share it via AirDrop, Files, WhatsApp, email, or any other app.

Can I recover deleted captures or visits?

No. Starting a new visit keeps your earlier visits, but anything you delete, or everything in the app if you delete the app, is gone for good. ScrapLog keeps your captures out of iCloud and computer backups, so export visits you want to keep.

What is ScrapLog Pro?

An optional subscription (monthly or yearly, with a 14-day free trial for new subscribers) that adds PDF reports with your own logo, unlimited visits, photo markup and comments, ISRI favorites and the volume-button shutter. Capturing, piles, grades and ZIP export stay free.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone, at least 24 hours before the period ends. Cancelling keeps all your data viewable and exportable. On a new device, tap "Restore purchases" on the ScrapLog Pro screen. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does ScrapLog work without an internet connection?

Yes — ScrapLog is fully offline. No internet connection is required to capture, organize, or export.

What data does ScrapLog collect?

None. ScrapLog has no backend, no analytics, and no account system. Your captures stay on your device. Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
